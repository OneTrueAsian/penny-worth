Penny Worth — before you install
=================================

When you run this installer, Windows will likely show a blue screen
that says "Windows protected your PC."

This is expected, and nothing is wrong — it just means this installer
isn't digitally signed with a certificate Windows already recognizes.
Penny Worth is not registered with Microsoft as a publisher.

To continue:

  1. Click "More info"
  2. Click "Run anyway"

That's it — install and open the app as normal from there.

One more thing worth knowing: Penny Worth runs entirely on your own
computer. There's no account, no cloud sync, and nothing is ever sent
anywhere else — your financial data stays on your machine, in a single
file, for as long as you have the app installed.
